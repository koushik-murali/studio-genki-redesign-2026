About Studio Genki - Pottery in Coimbatore

“All buildings are predictions. All predictions are wrong”

At its core, Studio Genki is an art and design studio in Coimbatore that aims to do high-quality, human-made ceramic work.

Operating Principles

  • Create thoughtful pieces: Work done with care and attention to detail.
  • Be like Sisyphus: Show up every day and push the boulder. The process is the reward.

About Koushik Murali

Koushik is a graphic designer and studio potter born in Coimbatore. Trained at Anvi Pottery under Master Potter Sandeep Manchekar and assisted John Dix during woodfiring workshops. Exposure to wood firing, gas firing, naked raku firing, soda firing, and lustre firing.
