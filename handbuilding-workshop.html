Handbuilding Pottery Classes in Coimbatore - Studio Genki

Studio Genki is an art & design studio in Coimbatore offering hands-on handbuilding pottery workshops and courses for all skill levels. Learn to make functional and decorative ceramic forms without a potter's wheel.

Courses & Pricing

  • Explore (6 Sessions / 18 hrs): ₹9,900. Includes 2 finished glazed pieces, 3 weeks validity. Best for beginners wanting a hands-on intro to clay sculpting and hand-formed pieces.
  • Foundation (10 Sessions / 30 hrs): ₹16,500. Most popular. Includes 5 finished glazed pieces, 5 weeks validity. Best for building coil, slab, and pinch technique mastery and surface texturing.
  • Intensive (20 Sessions / 60 hrs): ₹33,000. Includes 10 finished glazed pieces, 8 weeks validity, and unlimited free studio practice time within validity to establish an independent workflow.

What is Included

Unlimited clay for practice, sculpting tools, wooden ribs, texture mats, clean studio aprons, bisque and glaze firings, and 1-on-1 instruction capped at 3 students per slot.

Daily Schedule & Timings

Sessions run all days: Morning (10:00 AM - 1:00 PM), Afternoon (2:00 PM - 5:00 PM), Evening (5:30 PM - 8:30 PM). 3 hours per session.
