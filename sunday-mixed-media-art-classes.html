Sunday Mixed Media Art Classes — Painting | Ceramic Art | Craft

Learn painting, clay modeling and paper/texture work. Kid-friendly and beginner friendly. Weekly 2-hour Sunday classes for learners aged 10 years and above, taught by Karthik Subramanian (MFA, Government College of Arts, Chennai). Fee: Rs. 5,000 per month.

Class Structure (2 Hours)

  • Introduction and Concept Discussion: 10 min
  • Educator Demonstration: 15 min
  • Hands-on Practical Activity: 80 min
  • Individual Guidance: 10 min
  • Artwork Presentation and Feedback: 5 min

Learning Areas

  • Painting and Drawing: Basic drawing, colour theory, watercolour and acrylic painting, composition, texture exploration, still-life, landscape and abstract painting.
  • Ceramic Art and Clay Modelling: Clay preparation, hand-building, pinch pot, coil techniques, mugs, plates, bowls, underglaze painting.
  • Craft: Collage, paper craft, texture exploration, recycled material art, relief and surface-based artwork.
