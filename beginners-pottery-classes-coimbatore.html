Weekend Wheel Throwing Classes in Coimbatore - Studio Genki

Studio Genki is a dedicated pottery studio in Coimbatore offering an intensive 9-session weekend wheel throwing course for beginners and aspiring potters. No prior experience required.

Course Structure & Schedule

9 sessions across 5 weekends starting Saturday, October 24, 2026 and ending Saturday, November 21, 2026. 8 sessions dedicated to throwing & trimming practice and 1 dedicated session for glazing.

  • Week 1 (Oct 24 & 25): Bowls. Centering clay and making your first open bowls (practice only, pieces are not saved).
  • Week 2 (Oct 31 & Nov 1): Cylinders. Pulling straight walls and shaping taller cylinders (practice only, pieces are not saved).
  • Week 3 (Nov 7 & Nov 8): Bowls & Cylinders. Throwing foundation pieces to keep, and trimming foot rings on leather-hard ware.
  • Week 4 (Nov 14 & Nov 15): Mugs & Finished Ware. Throwing finished pieces, making coiled handles for mugs, final detailing and bisque prep.
  • Week 5 (Nov 21): Glazing session for all finished bowls, cylinders, and mugs.

Course Fee & Inclusions

₹16,500. Includes dedicated electric wheel, unlimited clay for practice drills, complete trimming tools, aprons, bisque firings, and cone 6 glaze firings. Capped strictly at 3 students per slot.
