Ceramic Art and Design Studio Based Out of Coimbatore - Studio Genki

Studio Genki is a dual-practice space in Coimbatore, dedicated to both small-batch ceramic production and an alternative, progressive design curriculum.

We offer beginner wheel throwing pottery classes, handbuilding ceramic courses, and weekend crash courses taught with dedicated 1-on-1 mentorship capped at 3 students per slot.

  • Beginners Wheel Throwing Pottery Course
  • Handbuilding Pottery Classes & Sculpting
  • Handcrafted Ceramic Tableware & Works
